# Implementation plan

## Product objective

Deliver one autonomous, governed vertical slice: a delegated campaign progresses from candidate discovery through an accepted meeting without routine human approvals. Every external effect is evidence-backed, ArmorIQ-authorized, input-bound, idempotent, verified, and audited.

## Delivery sequence

1. Establish tenant authentication, RBAC, workflow state, audit chaining, and a versioned policy envelope.
2. Implement the supervisor and specialized discovery, research, evidence, scoring, contact, strategy, outreach, conversation, scheduling, and meeting-preparation capabilities.
3. Wrap every consequential tool call in capture-purpose, commit-plan, ArmorIQ authorize, execute, verify, and audit stages.
4. Connect Google OAuth and governed Gmail, Calendar, Drive, and Docs adapters.
5. Add durable Cloud Tasks handlers, scheduler triggers, replay protection, retry limits, suppression, and cost/contact budgets.
6. Expose the mission, pipeline, escalations, policy decisions, run traces, audit verification, and analytics in the web application.
7. Test policy denial and permit paths, evidence mapping, prompt-injection resistance, idempotency, stop conditions, and a full autonomous account journey.
8. Validate local containers, Terraform, dependency scans, evaluations, migrations, deployed health, and rollback procedures.

## Definition of done

The system can autonomously secure a qualified first meeting inside a pre-delegated mission; cannot execute changed or unauthorized actions; stops on replies, unsubscribe, suppression, limits, or policy failure; exposes complete evidence and audit trails; and runs locally and in GCP from documented commands.
